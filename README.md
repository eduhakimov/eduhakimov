### 🇬🇧 English

I teach computer graphics at Andijan State University and research the **automation of 2D character animation in Adobe After Effects**: analytic inverse kinematics, keyframe-based secondary motion and error-bounded baking. I developed **ZiyoAnim IK**, an Uzbek-language After Effects module covered by 419 automated checks. Other interests: UI/UX usability (Sketch, Adobe XD, Figma, eye-tracking), image processing in MATLAB, and traffic simulation in SUMO GUI.

- 📄 **47 scientific papers** (2024–2026), most with open DOIs — [full list](https://eduhakimov.github.io)
- 📘 Co-author of the textbook **"Computer Graphics"** (2024, 162 pp., Ministry approval No. 571-11)
- 🧾 **6 registration certificates** for software and databases (Ministry of Justice of Uzbekistan)
- 🏅 **Beruniy State Scholarship** holder (2024/2025)
